Bake & Grill - Manual Location Search-First Flow

Customer flow:
1. The manual location modal opens with the area/village search field first.
2. Search suggestions appear below the search field while typing or after pressing Search.
3. Selecting a search result reveals the map centered on that selected area, with a draggable pin.
4. The customer drags the pin or taps the map to choose the exact home/delivery point.
5. Only after a real pin selection do the full address fields and Confirm button appear.
6. If search cannot find the place, the customer can use the fallback button to open the map and select a point manually.

The fallback map is initially centered near Bake & Grill but does NOT treat the shop as the customer's selected delivery location. The customer must move/tap the pin before address confirmation is enabled.

BAKE & GRILL CUSTOMER PHONE OTP

Checkout now uses Firebase Phone Authentication (SMS OTP) and requires a verified phone number before order submission. Customer profile is saved at customers/{uid}. Optional Order Instructions are saved on each order as orderInstructions. My Orders queries the signed-in phone number.

Firebase Console setup required:
1. Authentication > Sign-in method > enable Phone.
2. Authentication > Settings > Authorized domains: add bgpizza.github.io.
3. Authentication > Templates: check SMS region policy and allow India.
4. Ensure Firebase billing/SMS quota and phone verification configuration are active. Test phone numbers can be configured in Authentication > Sign-in method > Phone.
5. Firestore indexes may be required for orders query (phone ascending, createdAt descending).

OTP SMS is subject to Firebase phone auth availability, quotas, billing and reCAPTCHA. This package cannot enable Console settings automatically.

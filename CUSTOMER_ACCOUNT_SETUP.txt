BAKE & GRILL – AUTOMATIC CUSTOMER ACCOUNT

First order on a device/PWA:
1. Customer enters name + 10-digit phone once.
2. Firebase Anonymous Auth creates a passwordless customer session.
3. Profile is saved locally and in Firestore under customers/{uid}.
4. Future orders on the same browser/PWA use the saved name + phone automatically.

Important: this is passwordless and does not verify ownership of the phone number.
If you want the same account to work on a new phone/device, add Firebase Phone OTP authentication later.

Firebase Console requirement:
Authentication > Sign-in method > enable Anonymous.

BAKE & GRILL — MASTER LOUD NEW ORDER ALERT

Only the MASTER SITE was changed in this update. Customer-site files and customer ordering flow were not intentionally modified.

New-order behavior:
- Full-screen NEW ORDER overlay on the Master Site
- Loud repeating siren for new orders
- Accept Order stops the alert
- If the order is not accepted within 2 minutes, the existing cancellation flow runs
- Browser title changes to 🚨 NEW ORDER while the alert is active
- Master Site attempts to unlock browser audio on the first click/tap/key interaction to reduce autoplay blocking

Important: The Master Site must be open and logged in. Browser/OS volume must not be muted.

V33 CUSTOMER OTP FLOW
1. Customer enters name and 10-digit mobile number.
2. Click Continue; Firebase sends SMS OTP automatically.
3. Phone/name step hides and OTP entry screen appears.
4. Customer enters OTP and clicks Verify & Login.
5. For My Account login, successful verification opens order history automatically. For checkout, customer continues to place order.
6. Change mobile number returns to phone entry.
Firebase Phone Auth must be enabled; bgpizza.github.io must be an authorized domain and India (+91) SMS must be allowed.

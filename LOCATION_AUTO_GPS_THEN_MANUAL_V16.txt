BAKE & GRILL LOCATION FLOW V16

On first website load, the site requests device GPS automatically through the browser. The customer should choose Allow. If the customer denies/blocks permission, GPS is off/unavailable, or the request times out, the manual PIN + address + map-pin flow opens. No separate mobile-network/Wi-Fi location attempt is made. Customers can retry GPS from the manual flow.

Note: A website cannot switch the phone GPS setting ON by itself. Browser permission is required.

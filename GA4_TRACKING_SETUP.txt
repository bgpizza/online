BAKE & GRILL — GA4 ORDER TRACKING

GA4 Measurement ID: G-M2B7FN48S9
Source: Existing Firebase Web App configuration in firebase-config.js.

Tracked events:
- page_view: customer ordering page and standalone order tracking page
- view_item: when a customer opens a menu item
- add_to_cart: when a customer adds a configured item
- begin_checkout: when checkout is opened with items in cart
- purchase: only after the order is successfully saved to Firestore

Purchase event includes transaction_id, INR value and product details.
Customer name, phone number, delivery address and GPS coordinates are NOT sent to Google Analytics.

Google Business Profile attribution:
Use UTM-tagged links for website/menu/order links:
https://www.bakeandgrill.in/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=google_business_profile

Deployment:
Upload the CONTENTS of this online-main folder to the website repository's /online/ folder,
preserving all subfolders. Do not upload an extra nested online-main folder.

Verification:
After deployment, visit the website and test:
1. Open a menu item -> view_item
2. Add item -> add_to_cart
3. Open checkout -> begin_checkout
4. Place a test/real order -> purchase (only after Firestore save succeeds)

Check GA4 Admin > DebugView or Reports > Realtime. Events may take time to appear in standard reports.

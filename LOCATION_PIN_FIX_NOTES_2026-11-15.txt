Bake & Grill delivery location fixes (2026-11-15)

- Fixed AdvancedMarkerElement drag handling to listen for the documented `gmp-dragend` event.
- Kept map-tap selection: tapping the map relocates the delivery pin and reverse-geocodes the point.
- Added a required manualPinTouched guard: a PIN-code centroid cannot be confirmed as the customer's home until they drag the pin or tap their exact point on the map.
- PIN result selection opens the address form and map at zoom 19, with roadmap/satellite/hybrid map-type controls for checking narrow roads and building footprints where Google map data provides them.
- Reverse-geocoding now checks more road, premise, neighborhood and postal-town component types.
- Updated app.js cache-busting version to 20261115-gmp-dragend-pin-fix-v3.

Limitations:
- Google Maps cannot guarantee that every private lane, house number or building footprint exists in its map data. If no mapped address exists, the customer must place the pin manually.
- Live browser interaction, Google API billing/restrictions, Routes API and Firebase Authentication still require deployment-side testing.

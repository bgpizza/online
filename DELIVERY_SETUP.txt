BAKE & GRILL DELIVERY BOY SETUP

1. Firebase Console > Authentication > Users > Add user for each delivery boy.
2. Copy the user's UID.
3. Open Master page after login. In Delivery Partners section, enter UID, name, phone and Add/Update.
4. Give the delivery boy this URL: /online/delivery.html
5. Delivery boy logs in with the Authentication email/password.
6. When an order is OUT FOR DELIVERY, the assigned delivery boy sees it and can Start Live Tracking.
7. GPS location is written to Realtime Database at deliveryLocations/{uid}.
8. Customer tracking can be extended to read this location for the assigned order.

Realtime Database rules are in realtime-database.rules.json.
IMPORTANT: Do not use .read:true and .write:true in production.

The delivery PWA needs HTTPS (GitHub Pages works) and the phone must grant location permission.

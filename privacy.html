Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Bake & Grill (“we”, “us” or “our”), Sanjua–Bakhrahat, West Bengal, respects your privacy. This policy explains what information our website collects, why we use it and the choices available to you when you browse the site or place an order.

1. Information we collect

  • Account and contact details: your name and mobile number when you sign in or place an order. We use SMS one-time passwords (OTP) to verify the mobile number.
  • Order information: items, quantities, prices, order instructions, order status, and order date/time.
  • Delivery information: the address, landmark and delivery pin you provide, and the map coordinates you confirm or share through your device location permission. Location access can be denied; where available, you can enter an address and choose a map pin manually.
  • Notification information: a browser push-notification token, only when you allow notifications, so we can send order updates.
  • Technical and usage information: browser/device information and website interaction events collected through Google Analytics 4 and essential site technologies.

2. How we use information

  • Verify your mobile number and maintain your customer account.
  • Receive, prepare, deliver and track your order, including calculating delivery eligibility and road distance.
  • Contact you about an order, delivery clarification, support request or feedback.
  • Send order-status notifications when you opt in.
  • Understand site usage, troubleshoot errors, prevent misuse and improve the ordering experience.

3. Location permission and choices

We request device location only to help confirm the delivery destination and calculate delivery distance. You can refuse the browser location prompt and use the manual address/map-pin option. You can change the browser's location permission at any time in your device or browser settings. An order may still require a confirmed delivery address and map pin to establish whether delivery is available.

4. Service providers

We use Google/Firebase services for phone authentication, database storage, hosting-related application services and push notifications; Google Maps and OpenStreetMap-based routing services for maps and road-distance checks; Google reCAPTCHA for abuse prevention; and Google Analytics 4 for website usage measurement. These providers process relevant information to provide their services under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell customer personal information.

Optional advertising measurement

With your permission, we use Meta Pixel to measure Facebook and Instagram advertising and website interactions, including page views, product views, cart additions, checkout and submitted orders. Meta may receive browser and device information, cookies, product identifiers, order value and an event identifier. We do not intentionally send your name, phone number, delivery address or delivery coordinates through these events. Meta processes this information under its privacy policy. You may decline or change your choice using Advertising privacy choices on the ordering page; ordering remains available either way.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We share only the information needed with our staff and service providers to accept, prepare and deliver your order, operate the website, protect the service or comply with law. Delivery address and map information are used for fulfilment and are not intended to be publicly displayed. We may disclose information where legally required or necessary to protect customers, the business or the public.

6. Storage, security and retention

Account, order and delivery information is stored using Firebase services. We apply access controls intended to restrict customer records to the relevant customer and authorised Bake & Grill staff. No internet service can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain records for as long as reasonably necessary for order fulfilment, customer support, business records, security and legal obligations, after which they may be deleted or anonymised where appropriate.

7. Your choices and requests

You may deny or change location and notification permissions in your browser/device settings. You can sign out of your customer account on the device. To request access to, correction of or deletion of your account or personal information, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify that you own the mobile number before acting on a request and may retain information where required for legitimate business or legal purposes.

8. Children's privacy

Our online ordering service is intended to be used by adults placing orders. Children should use the service with the involvement of a parent or guardian.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or legal requirements change. The latest version and update date will be published on this page.

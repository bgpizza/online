Bake & Grill — Estimated Time Rule

Base preparation time: 20 minutes
Delivery time: road distance (OSRM) × 6 minutes per KM

Order 1:
  Estimated = 20 + (road KM × 6)

Order 2 and every later order:
  Estimated = 20 + 20 buffer + (road KM × 6)

Examples at 3 KM:
  Order 1 = 38 minutes
  Order 2+ = 58 minutes

Important: the buffer is based ONLY on the global order number. It does not inspect customer name, account, phone number, loyalty points, or customer profile.

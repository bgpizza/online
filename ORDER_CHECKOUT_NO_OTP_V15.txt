BAKE & GRILL V15 — SIMPLE CHECKOUT

- Removed customer SMS OTP flow from checkout.
- Customer enters name and 10-digit mobile number, then taps Proceed Order.
- Uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication in the background to support authenticated Firestore writes.
- Does not automatically request GPS or network/Wi-Fi location on page load. Customer must select/confirm delivery location manually; road-distance and delivery-area checks remain required.
- Master panel remains at /online/master.html and requires the existing Firebase Email/Password admin login.

Firebase requirements:
1. Authentication > Sign-in method: Anonymous must be Enabled. (Phone sign-in is no longer needed for customer orders.)
2. Authentication > Sign-in method: Email/Password must remain enabled for Master Panel login.
3. Firestore rules must permit signed-in (including anonymous) users to create order and publicStatuses documents, and customers to write their own customer profile as configured.

Master URL: https://bgpizza.github.io/online/master.html
